World Name: Earth (Perfect Alternative)

Core Concept:
Our world, Earth, in the modern era. All nations, cities, languages, cultures, social behaviors, economic systems, technologies, history, and every other aspect of daily life are ABSOLUTELY IDENTICAL to the Earth we live in. The only difference is that the entire "human" population in this world consists of Beastmen with specific physical traits agreed upon. Their animal characteristics are purely visual and hold no other biological, social, or physiological influence.

Beastmen on Earth (Perfect Alternative):
● Physical Appearance: Every individual is a Beastman. They possess a humanoid posture identical to humans (upright bipedal walking, two dexterous hands).
- Fur Animals: For species based on fur-bearing animals, their entire bodies are covered in neat, smooth, short fur (shorthair).
- Scalies & Aquatic Species: For reptile, amphibian, or aquatic-based species, their skin texture is regular smooth skin identical to human skin; they DO NOT have scales.
- Heads: Their heads correspond to their animal archetype (wolf, cat, eagle, lizard, fish, etc.).
- Critical Rule: ABSOLUTELY NO TAILS.

● Species Diversity and Distribution: The diversity of Beastmen species reflects the entire animal kingdom on Earth. However, their geographical distribution is completely random and has zero connection to the original habitats of those animals. You can find Panda Beastmen in Indonesia, Penguin Beastmen in Egypt, Camel Beastmen in Siberia, or Frog Beastmen in a dry metropolitan area. They are simply citizens of the country where they were born and raised, period.

● Size: Their body sizes are uniform within the normal range of human sizes.

Implications on Earth (Perfect Alternative):
1. Language, Culture, Behavior, Economy, Technology:
   - All these aspects are 100% identical to our Earth. There are no cultural, linguistic, behavioral, economic, or technological adaptations due to the existence of Beastmen. How they interact, their values, how they work, and the tools they use are exactly the same as humans in our world.
   - A Polar Bear Beastman born and raised in Japan will speak fluent Japanese, follow Japanese customs, and behave as a Japanese person. Their physical appearance as a Polar Bear is irrelevant to their cultural identity.

2. Professions and Capabilities:
   - All professions are open to all types of Beastmen without exception. There are no inherent advantages or disadvantages based on animal archetype. A Mouse Beastman can be a champion powerlifter, and an Eagle Beastman can be a deep-sea diver. Success in any career is determined solely by individual talent, education, training, and hard work, just like in our world. Everyone is equal.

3. Identity:
   - Their animal features (head type, shorthair fur, smooth non-scale skin) are purely visual characteristics, much like hair or eye color among humans in our world—perhaps even less significant. It does not define their identity, does not affect how society views them (beyond basic visual recognition), and carries no social or cultural stereotypes. Their identity is shaped entirely by nationality, culture, personality, and personal life choices.

4. Social Harmony:
   - There are no social conflicts, prejudices, or discrimination (neither subtle nor overt) based on Beastmen species. Society is completely "species-blind." Everyone is treated equally under the law and in social interactions.

5. Biology and Physiology:
   - Their diet is identical to human diet and is determined by culture and personal preference, not by their animal type. A Lion Beastman can be a strict vegetarian, a Shark Beastman can enjoy fruit salad, and a Cow Beastman can love eating beef steaks. Their digestive physiology is uniform and omnivorous like standard humans.
   - Medical needs and general biological functions are also uniform and identical to humans.

6. Livestock and Wildlife:
   - This world also has non-Beastmen livestock (cattle, chickens, pigs, etc.) and wild animals, exactly like our Earth. The presence of these animals has no special connection or relationship to Beastmen, regardless of a Beastman's visual appearance. A Chicken Beastman views farm chickens simply as poultry/food, just like a human would in our world. There is no sense of kinship or specific conflict.

Summary:
Imagine our modern Earth with zero changes in how society, culture, or daily life operates—except that everyone physically appears as a tailless Beastman with diverse animal heads, shorthair fur for fur types, and regular smooth skin (no scales) for scalies. These animal traits are nothing more than cosmetic visual variations.
