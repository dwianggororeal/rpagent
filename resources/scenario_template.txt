================================================================================
                    INITIAL SCENARIO TEMPLATE (STORY & SCENARIO SETUP)
================================================================================

1. CORE PREMISE (The Core Hook)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[1-2 sentences summary: Who meets whom, in what situation, and what major conflict is unfolding.]


2. TIME, PLACE & ATMOSPHERE (Setting)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Specific Location : [e.g., Underground harbor corridor, abandoned apartment, coffee shop during a storm]
- Time & Weather    : [e.g., Midnight at 02:00 AM, heavy rain and thick fog]
- Atmosphere / Mood : [e.g., Tense, cold, frantic, suspenseful, or casual yet awkward]


3. INITIAL DYNAMIC (Relationship at the Start)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[What is the relationship dynamic between the two characters when the scene begins?
Examples:
- Two complete strangers who accidentally collide while fleeing
- Enemies/rivals forced to take shelter in the same room
- A victim rescued by a mysterious stranger
- A superior and subordinate trapped in a sudden crisis]


4. INCITING INCIDENT (The Trigger / First Encounter)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[What concrete event causes them to meet or ignites this opening scene?
e.g., One character breaks in wounded, or both are scrambling to secure the same piece of vital evidence.]


5. WHY THEY CANNOT WALK AWAY (The Binding Factor)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[Why CANNOT they simply leave or walk away from each other right now?
e.g., The exits are locked from the outside, one holds crucial information the other desperately needs, or they are surrounded by hostile forces.]


6. THE STAKES & IMMEDIATE DANGER (High Stakes)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Immediate Danger : [What direct threat happens right now if they fail to act?]
- High Stakes      : [What catastrophic long-term consequence looms over them if this entire situation fails?]


7. SCENE GOAL VS MAIN STORY GOAL (Target & Trajectory)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Immediate Goal   : [What must be resolved within this opening scene? e.g., treat a wound, escape the building, hide the contraband]
- Main Story Goal  : [Where will their relationship and overarching plot head after this introductory scene?]


8. OPENING SCENE SETUP / STARTER PROMPT (First Scene Hook)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[Draft a 1-paragraph opening prompt to launch the story/roleplay: describe the initial situation, physical positions of both characters, and the first action that takes place.]
